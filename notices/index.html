Here I explain what data this site uses, who owns the trademarks that appear on it and what you can do with its content.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, analytics or forms, so I receive no data from your visit.

Your browser keeps three pieces of data on your own device. The theme you picked (theme), the advancements you found (ol-adv) and whether you turned off the chat shortcut (ol-keys). Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

To show the current Skylease version and check whether it is on Modrinth yet, your browser asks the public GitHub and Modrinth APIs. Those requests show them your IP address, the same as visiting their sites. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visits under GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you write to me on Discord, that conversation stays on Discord and follows its rules.
