Skylease 1.0.0 - Build 1

Per-player flight time for Paper servers.

Paper 1.21+ · Java 21 · Apache-2.0

Download SourceModrinth

How it works

Every player has their own flight time. /fly starts flying and the time counts down while they fly, and when it runs out, the player glides down to the ground so they do not fall all at once.

Paper 1.21 or later, Java 21. PlaceholderAPI and WorldGuard are optional.

  • 33 particle effects, each with its own permission
  • A WorldGuard flag to keep flight out of certain regions
  • Placeholders for PlaceholderAPI
  • SQLite out of the box, or MySQL/MariaDB to share data between servers
  • In English and Spanish, with room for your own language

Commands

CommandDoes
/flyTurns your flight on or off
/fly settingsOpens the alerts and effects menu
/flyadmin add <player> <time>Adds time
/flyadmin remove <player> [time]Takes time away, or all of it if you give no amount
/flyadmin info <player>Shows a player's data
/flyadmin reloadReloads the configuration
/skylease aboutShows the credits

Times are written with s, m, h, d, mo and y, alone or combined, like 30s, 2h or 1h30m.

Details

Tested

I tested it on Paper 1.21.11 with Java 21 and 26. Flight, the menus, both languages, the WorldGuard flag, PlaceholderAPI and MySQL through MariaDB all work.

I haven't tested it on Folia yet, or on networks where a player moves between servers.

Data

Per player it stores the UUID, name, time left, chosen effect and alerts. The update checker makes one request to the GitHub API on start-up and sends nothing about your server.
