#001

Skylease

Released  Paper 1.21+ · Java 21 · 1.0.0 - Build 1

Every player has their own flight time. /fly starts flying and the time counts down while they fly, and when it runs out, the player glides down to the ground so they do not fall all at once.

  • 33 particle effects, each with its own permission
  • A WorldGuard flag to keep flight out of certain regions
  • Placeholders for PlaceholderAPI
  • SQLite out of the box, or MySQL/MariaDB to share data between servers
  • In English and Spanish, with room for your own language

#002

Skywave

In development  Paper 1.21+ · Java 21

A radio for survival servers that works only through commands. You tune a frequency, talk, and anyone nearby on the same band hears you. The range of the signal depends on how high you are, the weather and whether there is a roof over you, since I wanted it to feel like a real radio.

Code, tests and two-player test done. Icon, screenshots and page still to come.

About me

33particle effects in Skylease
11unit tests in Skywave
2languages per plugin
1.21+minimum Paper version

I started making plugins in my free time, for small servers, without really knowing how a real project gets published. Those first plugins worked, but I did not feel they were ready to show, so now I am rewriting them one by one and calmly, because I want them to turn out well.

Before I publish anything, I test it on a local server to check that every feature does what it is supposed to do. The parts that can fail for very specific reasons, like language files or the way times are read, each have their own test to prevent it. I put a lot of effort into making everything reach people in the best possible shape, since I have a perfectionism that eats at me far too much.

My interest in Law also shows in my plugins. For Skylease I wrote an addendum to the Apache-2.0 licence that asks for the credits to stay where they are, since it matters to me that everyone's work is recognised. Thanks to that, I also started helping communities with their rules and legal documents.
